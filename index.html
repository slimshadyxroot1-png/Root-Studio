Browser

Playlist Click bars to paint the selected pattern (a long pattern fills its length). Click a bar number to set the loop start, Shift+click for the end. Drag in the lane under the bars to automate in song mode.
Channel rack
Drum sounds Drag in the lane to set the velocity of each hit. Pan, reverb, delay and effects are on the drum's mixer strip.
Mixer Pan, reverb and delay sends, mute and solo per strip. Master EQ, reverb and drive on the right.
Audio tracks Click a track name to choose where audio goes. Move: drag a clip, or drag its edges to trim. Cut: click a clip. Stretch: drag a clip longer or shorter (pitch follows).
Synthesizer Drives the selected track. Osc 1 is chosen in the piano roll.
Automation Drag to draw a value per step in this pattern. Shift+drag erases. The dashed line is the knob position.
